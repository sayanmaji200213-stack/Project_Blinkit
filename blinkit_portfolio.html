01 · Project Overview

What This Project Does

This dashboard was built to simulate a real-world analytics workflow for Blinkit, India's instant grocery delivery platform. Starting from publicly available raw datasets in CSV format, the project covers the entire BI pipeline — from raw data ingestion and transformation in Power Query, through relational data modelling, all the way to interactive, insight-driven dashboards for business decision-makers.

The four report pages are designed for distinct business stakeholder audiences: marketing teams tracking campaign ROI, operations reviewing customer health metrics, and leadership reviewing revenue forecasts — all unified under Blinkit's brand identity.

🎯 Problem Statement

Raw operational data across orders, customers, inventory, delivery, marketing, and feedback was siloed across 11 separate files with inconsistent formats, missing fields, and no relational structure.

⚙️ Approach

Cleaned and transformed all 11 sources using Power Query, merged datasets into analytical views, built a star schema with a Calendar table, and authored DAX measure groups for each domain.

📊 Output

Four polished report pages with slicers, KPI cards, combination charts, map visuals, decomposition trees, and time-series forecasting — all wired through a single coherent data model.

📦 Dataset Scope

~5,000 orders, ~5,000 customers, ~5,400 marketing campaign rows, ~5,000 delivery and feedback records, 268 products across multiple categories — spanning 2023–2024.

04 · DAX Measures

Key Measures & Calculations

Measures were organised into three dedicated measure tables — isolating marketing, customer, and forecasting logic. A What-If Parameter enables dynamic ROAS target adjustment directly in the report.

Measure Group Description
ROAS Marketing Return on Ad Spend — Total Revenue divided by Total Spend. Displayed on a gauge against a configurable target ROAS parameter.
Total Revenue Marketing Sum of revenue generated across all marketing campaigns. Used in gauge and combo chart.
Total Spend Marketing Sum of campaign spend. Paired with Total Revenue on combo chart to visualise spend efficiency over time.
Total Impressions Marketing Aggregate campaign impressions — top-of-funnel reach metric.
Total Clicks Marketing Total clicks across all campaigns and channels.
Total Conversions Marketing Count of successful conversions — drives decomposition tree analysis.
Number of Campaigns Marketing Distinct count of campaign IDs active in the selected period.
Total Sales Customer Sum of order_total from the orders fact table. Primary revenue KPI on the Customer page.
Total Orders Customer Count of distinct orders. Used in map visual and KPI card.
AOV Customer Average Order Value — Total Sales divided by Total Orders. Geographic variation shown on filled map.
Customer Sat % Customer Derived from feedback ratings — percentage of orders with positive sentiment or high ratings (4–5 stars).
OnTime % Customer Percentage of deliveries completed on or before the promised delivery time — sourced from delivery_performance table.
Target ROAS Value Parameter What-If Parameter allowing end users to set a custom ROAS benchmark dynamically via a slider, compared against actual ROAS on gauge.

05 · Data Transformation

Power Query (M) — ETL Pipeline

All 11 raw CSV files were transformed in Power Query Editor before being loaded into the data model. Key transformation steps included:

01

Data Type Enforcement

Corrected data types across all tables — parsing date-time columns, converting numeric strings, and enforcing integer/decimal distinctions on price, margin, and delivery time fields.

02

Column Cleaning & Renaming

Standardised column names for consistency across tables, removed redundant or duplicate columns, and trimmed whitespace from categorical fields (area, segment, channel, payment_method).

03

Null Handling

Filled or filtered null values — particularly in reasons_if_delayed (delivery table) and feedback text columns. Maintained null integrity in foreign key fields to preserve relationship accuracy.

04

Merge Queries — orders_extended

Left-joined blinkit_orders with blinkit_customers to produce an enriched order table (orders_extended) containing customer segment, area, and pincode alongside order fields — used on the Customer page.

05

Merge Queries — order_items_extended

Joined blinkit_order_items with blinkit_products to bring category, product name, and brand into the line-item level — enabling category-level sales analysis and top-product ranking.

06

Inventory Consolidation

Appended blinkit_inventory and blinkit_inventoryNew into a single unified inventory table to represent the full stock movement timeline without data gaps.

07

Date Column Extraction

Extracted date-only columns from datetime fields (e.g., Order Date Only, Campaign Date) to support relationship creation with the Calendar table and enable consistent time-intelligence slicing.

07 · Tools & Methods

Technical Stack

Power BI Desktop

Primary development environment for report authoring, visual design, data modelling, and DAX calculation.

Power Query (M Language)

ETL layer for cleaning, transforming, merging, and shaping all 11 raw CSV sources into analysis-ready tables.

DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)

Used for all calculated measures across three measure tables — including time intelligence, ratio calculations, and conditional aggregations.

Star Schema Modelling

Relational data model design with fact and dimension tables, a generated Calendar table, and cross-table filter propagation.

What-If Parameters

Dynamic parameter table enabling user-controlled ROAS target threshold — interactive business goal-setting within the report.

Power BI Forecasting

Native time-series forecasting applied to revenue and conversion metrics — providing forward-looking trend projections on the Forecast page.
